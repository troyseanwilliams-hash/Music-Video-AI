# Photo animation demo — executed prompts

## Still-image prompt

Cinematic photorealistic wide 16:9 landscape film still. An original adult woman explorer in a muted teal rain jacket stands on a wooden lakeside dock holding a small warm brass lantern at twilight. Seen three-quarter back view, natural face mostly turned toward the lake, shoulder-length dark hair. Serene mist rolls across a mountain lake, pine forest silhouettes and distant violet mountains, subtle firefly-like points of light over the water. Strong stable composition, explorer placed on left third with clear dock and water filling center, realistic hands and anatomy, quiet wonder, restrained indigo teal and amber palette, high-end film photography, natural lens and light. No text, logos, signs or watermarks. Suitable as the starting frame of a gentle six-second camera push-in with rippling water, drifting mist and softly moving hair.

## Image-to-video prompt

Animate the supplied image as one continuous six-second cinematic shot. Preserve the explorer, her clothing, lantern, dock, lake and composition. Slow gentle camera push toward the explorer and the lake. Fine mist drifts laterally over subtle water ripples; her hair and jacket move slightly in a light breeze; the lantern glows steadily. The explorer remains mostly still, making only a small natural head turn toward the distant light. Restrained realistic motion, no new objects or people, no scene cuts, no text, no logos, no face changes. End with the original composition still recognizable.
