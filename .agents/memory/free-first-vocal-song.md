---
name: Free first vocal song
description: Lifetime vocal-song trial entitlement and its billing-safety boundaries.
---

Every non-admin account receives one lifetime free vocal song. This is separate from recurring instrumental Soundtrack allowances and must not reset with subscription cycles.

**Why:** Vocal songs became more expensive, so the user chose one free successful song as the introductory experience.

**How to apply:** Claim the entitlement atomically before paid provider work so concurrent requests cannot both use it. Restore the claim after any failed generation or delivery attempt. A successful free vocal song must not debit credits, increment credit usage, or consume the instrumental allowance.
