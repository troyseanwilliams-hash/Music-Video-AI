---
name: Narrative text-only continuity
description: Durable product boundary between Narrative Story continuity and reference-capable generation modes.
---

New Narrative Story jobs accept one natural-language script or premise and derive every chronological scene from it for the selected duration. They must not require numbered scene lines or condition generation on uploaded, gallery-selected, or generated character-anchor images.

**Why:** Image-reference conditioning produced inconsistent characters and made the workflow too complicated. Narrative Story now uses a repeated text character bible while keeping source-image preview and approval.

**How to apply:** Keep Narrative Story reference inputs hidden and ignored at both client and server boundaries. Treat each source story beat's cast as authoritative: environment-only beats stay character-free, and generated prompt enrichment must be discarded when it changes character role, gender, or multiplicity. Keep persisted older jobs compatible, and do not change Montage or image-pack reference behavior when modifying shared planning or processing code.
