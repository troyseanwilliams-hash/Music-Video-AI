---
name: Lyrics topic separation
description: Keeps song subject matter distinct from production style in the Soundtracks workflow.
---

AI lyric writing must collect a dedicated topic or story brief instead of silently reusing the music style and vibe prompt.

**Why:** A production prompt describes how a track should sound, not what its lyrics should say. Reusing it creates generic or off-topic writing and makes the control misleading.

**How to apply:** Keep lyric subject, music style, and editable lyrics as separate inputs. Require a subject before generating fresh lyrics; enhancement may use the subject to preserve intent.
