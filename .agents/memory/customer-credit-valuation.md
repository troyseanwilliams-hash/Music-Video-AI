name: Customer credit valuation
description: Confirmed dollar value of customer-facing credits and how it affects provider-cost pricing.
---

Customer-facing credits are valued at $0.01 each: 1,000 credits equals $10. The intended Runway pricing markup is 2×. Existing customer prices are a floor and must never be reduced by recalculation.

**Why:** The prior calculator assumption of $0.015 per credit understates the number of customer credits required to cover direct provider spend and the intended markup. The user explicitly requires pricing changes to protect or improve revenue, never lower current prices.

**How to apply:** Convert provider cost and the 2× markup into customer credits using $0.01 per credit, then charge the greater of the existing price or the newly calculated price. For direct Runway Gen-4.5 at 12 credits per second, a 10-second scene has a 240-app-credit target price. Verify the current official per-second rate before changing prices.
