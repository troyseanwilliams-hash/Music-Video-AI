---
name: Customer credit valuation
description: Confirmed dollar value of customer-facing credits and how it affects provider-cost pricing.
---

Customer-facing credits are valued at $0.01 each: 1,000 credits equals $10.

**Why:** The prior calculator assumption of $0.015 per credit understates the number of customer credits required to cover direct provider spend and the intended markup.

**How to apply:** Convert provider cost and markup into customer credits using $0.01 per credit. For direct Runway Gen-4.5, verify the current official per-second rate before changing prices.
