Lead new-customer discovery with Montage and explain Generate as the credit-priced next step, without adding new access restrictions.

**Why:** The user wants customers to experience useful creation during the trial before paying for new AI scenes. Leading with Generate sends zero-credit trial accounts to a paid workflow.

**How to apply:** Keep trial allowances distinct from generation costs. Preserve explicit Montage or Generate destinations through signup. Prompt examples should support both story summaries and chronological numbered input without implying video guarantees one clip per line.
