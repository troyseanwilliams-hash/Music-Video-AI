---
name: Audio analysis presentation
description: Product decision for presenting BPM, mood, key, and energy analysis to customers.
---

Audio analysis is supporting intelligence for music-video timing and scene pacing, not a standalone customer-facing product or post-render report.

**Why:** A static “Audio Profile” did not help users complete their creative workflow and made the product feel focused on analysis rather than finished creator content.

**How to apply:** Keep using analysis behind the scenes where it improves beat matching, timing, and storyboards. When explaining the capability, show it visually alongside a music-video example rather than restoring a separate profile card.
