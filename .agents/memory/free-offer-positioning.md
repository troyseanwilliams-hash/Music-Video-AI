name: Free offer positioning
description: Distinguish the free Montage trial from paid AI generation and Stripe subscriptions.
---

Lead with “Get 150 free credits when you create your account,” not “7-day free trial” or zero-credit wording. Put the image/video usage example and the seven-day welcome-credit expiry beside the primary signup CTA.

**Why:** The user replaced separate introductory generation giveaways with one credit allowance so prospects can try a video or images directly.

**How to apply:** Keep basic Montage access available under existing trial/post-trial rules after credits run out. Music, lyric export, and Auto-sync introductory freebies must not be added on top. Preserve existing paid-plan benefits. Paid checkout charges immediately and does not repeat the welcome grant; keep Suite claims separate.

Welcome credits must stay separate from purchased and subscription credits, expire with the original signup trial, and never become permanent through a refund after expiry. Preserve previously issued non-expiring welcome grants.

**Why:** The user changed the offer from 110 non-expiring credits to 150 expiring credits. Retroactively confiscating old grants or purchased credits was not authorized.

**How to apply:** Maintain source-aware reservation and refund metadata through all API and worker generation paths. Consume expiring welcome credits before paid credits; account and balance reads must exclude expired balances.

Prioritize a simple first-video experience that helps new users spend their existing welcome credits, while keeping advanced creation available.

**Why:** The user said Generate Video is too complex and requested a simple version to promote usage of free credits, not a larger giveaway.

**How to apply:** Recommend an affordable complete configuration, including image costs, and preserve explicit approval before spending. Do not add entitlements to simplify onboarding.
