---
name: Free offer positioning
description: Distinguish the free Montage trial from paid AI generation and Stripe subscriptions.
---

Call the free offer a Montage trial, not an unrestricted AI-generation trial. Zero starting generation credits alone does not justify removing a genuine free trial label.

**Why:** The punch list flagged zero credits as contradictory, but the account still provides useful free Montage access and separate lifetime music allowances. Paid Stripe checkout is not the start of a delayed-charge trial.

**How to apply:** Verify current entitlements before updating marketing copy; explain free outputs and paid AI requirements near signup CTAs. Do not change economic rules merely to simplify copy. Keep AIBeatSync and Suite billing claims separate.
