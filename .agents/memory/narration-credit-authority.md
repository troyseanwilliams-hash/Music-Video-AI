name: Narration credit authority
description: Product boundary for charging script-to-narration work inside AIBeatSync.
---

AIBeatSync narration should mirror AIPodSync’s core script-to-episode experience while reserving and refunding AIBeatSync credits, not AIPodSync or Suite podcast credits.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose AIBeatSync credits for narration, keeping each product’s credit authority clear even when their creative workflows converge.

**How to apply:** Any future narration pricing, retries, refunds, top-ups, receipts, or usage history inside AIBeatSync must use the AIBeatSync subscription/top-up buckets and existing atomic billing rules.
